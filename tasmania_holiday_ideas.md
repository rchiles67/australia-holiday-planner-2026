## 📍 Route Overview & Key Highlights

This collection of holiday ideas is compiled from a 10-day campervan road trip looping anti-clockwise around Tasmania, starting and finishing in Hobart. The anti-clockwise route allows you to experience the dramatic east coast beaches first before heading inland and through the wild west coast mountains.

```
Hobart ➔ Tasman Peninsula ➔ East Coast & Freycinet ➔ Bay of Fires ➔ Launceston ➔ North Coast / Stanley ➔ Cradle Mountain ➔ West Coast (Strahan & Queenstown) ➔ Hobart
```

## 💡 Practical Travel Tips from the Trip

1. **National Parks Pass:**
   * A valid Tasmania National Parks Pass is strictly required. A **2-Month Holiday Pass** costs around **~$100 AUD per vehicle** and covers all national parks (Freycinet, Cradle Mountain, Narawntapu, etc.). Keep it on display in your vehicle windshield.
2. **Cradle Mountain Transport Logistics:**
   * Private vehicles / campervans are restricted inside Cradle Mountain NP during shuttle operating hours.
   * You must park at the Visitor Center and ride the official shuttle bus (included with your National Parks Pass).
3. **Campground Apps:**
   * For campervan road trips, use **WikiCamps Australia** or the **THL App** to locate free campgrounds (e.g. Cozy Corner), dump points, and powered caravan sites.
4. **Wildlife Watching:**
   * **Wombats:** Best seen around Ronny Creek (Cradle Mountain) late in the afternoon or at dusk.
   * **Little Penguins:** Best seen on Godfreys Beach shoreline in Stanley after sunset (around 8:30 PM in summer).
